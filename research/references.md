# Project References

This file compiles the authoritative sources, intelligence frameworks, and documentation standards used to construct NovaCore's threat intelligence business profile, regional threat context, and priority collection strategies.

## 1. Government & Regulatory Intelligence
* **Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA):** Consulted for emerging critical infrastructure alerts, active ransomware campaigns, and standardized response workflows via the [CISA Cybersecurity Advisories Repository](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories).
* **Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI):** Referenced for Joint Cyber Security Advisories (JCAs) outlining North American financial fraud models and advanced ransomware threat actors.

## 2. Industry Threat Intelligence Reports
* **Microsoft Threat Intelligence:** Used to track active fintech, cryptocurrency, and financial institution threat groupings, as outlined via the [Microsoft Defender Threat Intelligence platform](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/security/business/siem-and-xdr/microsoft-defender-threat-intelligence) and ongoing global risk briefings.
* **CrowdStrike Global Threat Intelligence:** Referenced for real-time tracking of adversaries (e.g., Scattered Spider / UNC3944), initial access broker dynamics, and credential harvesting methodologies across the [CrowdStrike Security Platform](https://crowdstrike.com).

## 3. Frameworks & Standardized Data Ontologies
* **MITRE ATT&CK® Enterprise Matrix:** Integrated to map tactical threat actors directly to standard cyberattack tactics, techniques, and specific IDs (e.g., Phishing T1566) within corporate financial perimeters.
* **FS-ISAC (Financial Services Information Sharing and Analysis Center):** Modeled as a baseline framework for peer-to-peer threat intelligence exchange within the regional fintech ecosystem.
